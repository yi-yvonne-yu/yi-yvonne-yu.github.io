About me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at Purdue University, advised by Dr. Yuxiang Peng.

Previously, I was a Master's student in Computer Science and Engineering at The Ohio State University, advised by Dr. Sachin Kumar. Before joining OSU, I conducted research at Academia Sinica with Dr. Chuan-Ju Wang and completed my undergraduate studies at National Chengchi University under the supervision of Dr. Ming-Feng Tsai.

My research interests lie in Natural Language Processing, Information Retrieval, Large Language Models, and AI for Math, with a focus on improving their efficiency, reliability, and deployment in real-world applications.

Research interests

  • Natural Language Processing

  • Large Language Models

Research

I work at the intersection of language modeling and information access, with an emphasis on systems that remain useful when queries are complex, evidence is sparse, or reliability matters.

  • Retrieval-Augmented Generation

    Grounding model outputs in relevant evidence for safer, more dependable answers.

  • Multi-query Retrieval

    Using diverse query representations and late fusion to improve retrieval coverage.

  • High-stakes Evaluation

    Designing benchmarks for rare and off-guideline questions in medical QA.

  • Efficient AI

    Making retrieval and language-model pipelines efficient enough for practical deployment.
