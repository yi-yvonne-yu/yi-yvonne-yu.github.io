About me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at Purdue University, advised by Dr. Yuxiang Peng.

Previously, I was a Master's student in Computer Science and Engineering at The Ohio State University, advised by Dr. Sachin Kumar. Before joining OSU, I conducted research at Academia Sinica with Dr. Chuan-Ju Wang and completed my undergraduate studies at National Chengchi University under the supervision of Dr. Ming-Feng Tsai.

My research interests lie in Natural Language Processing, Information Retrieval, Large Language Models, and AI for Math, with a focus on improving their efficiency, reliability, and deployment in real-world applications.

Research interests

  • Natural Language Processing

    Building reliable methods for understanding and generating language.

  • Information Retrieval

    Improving retrieval, ranking, query expansion, and retrieval-augmented generation.

  • Large Language Models

    Studying efficient and trustworthy LLM systems for real-world use.

  • AI for Math

    Exploring reasoning and retrieval methods for mathematical problem solving.
