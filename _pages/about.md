I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at Purdue University, where I am advised by [Dr. Yuxiang Peng](https://pickspeng.github.io/). Previously, I was a Master's student in Computer Science and Engineering at The Ohio State University (OSU), where I was advised by [Dr. Sachin Kumar](https://sites.google.com/view/sachinkumar). Before joining OSU, I conducted research at Academia Sinica with [Dr. Chuan-Ju Wang](http://cfda.csie.org/~cjwang/) and completed my undergraduate studies at National Chengchi University under the supervision of [Dr. Ming-Feng Tsai](https://www.cs.nccu.edu.tw/~mftsai/).

My research interests lie in Natural Language Processing (NLP), Information Retrieval (IR), Large Language Models (LLMs), and AI for Math, with a focus on improving their efficiency, reliability, and deployment in real-world applications.
